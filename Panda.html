Lazy River Pandamonium is an online co-op shooter starring 2 pandas. The players can choose to play as the Giant Panda in the center or the Red Panda orbitting the center. The center panda moves the float while the orbitting panda shoots scissors. The players goal is to reach the end of the lazy river while popping enemy floats and staying afloat themselves. The players can also push enemies into posts to destroy both of them and spawn a health item.

Lazy River Pandamonium was my first multiplayer game! I had no experience with netcode prior, and was the main programmer on the team. Production therefore consumed most of my time during the jam. While I try to iterate on my games' designs as much as possible, there was too little time to do so meanginfully during this jam. While the game itself is fun, I think it has much more potential than we were able to realize. I am proud of the final result given the technical challenge, and will be able to execute on multiplayer code faster next time.
