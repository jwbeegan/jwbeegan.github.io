MechAvenger

MechAvenger, a VR shooter platformer, released on Steam January 10th, 2024

MechAvenger is my first game, not counting game jams and student projects. This was a solo project, so I was responsible for the coding, design, music and SFX. MechAvenger focuses on fast paced VR shooter combat as well as challenging climbing and platforming. You can find out more about MechAvenger here.
