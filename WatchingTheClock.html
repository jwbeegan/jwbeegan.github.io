Watching the clock is a game about constructing watches within the time limit. The player must maneuver the pieces into the watch body without breaking them. If the watch fits the required design, it sells for money. Each day the amount of money the player needs to make increases.

Watching The Clock was my first time leading a team for a game jam. While I had experience in leadership in other fields, this was a first for me. It was also my return to game jams after a long hiatus. I learned many valuable lessons about designing a compelling game and leading a team of individuals. The actual design of the game leaves much to be desired, and we had to pivot the design heavily in the final hours of the jam. Nonetheless, I am proud of the final product.
